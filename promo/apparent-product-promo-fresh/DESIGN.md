# Apparent Product Promo

## Style Prompt

Create a polished vertical product promo for Apparent that feels like a launch film for a serious founder tool. The visual language should be product-led motion graphics: command line, signal extraction, proof profile, investor heat map, thesis matching, and warm intro. It should feel sharper and more complete than an illustration reel.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f4efe6`
- Ink: `#18140f`
- Charcoal: `#2a2520`
- Ember: `#d46132`
- Olive: `#7f9f5d`
- Sky: `#6b9eb8`
- Cream: `#fffaf0`

## Typography

- Display: `Georgia`, heavy, tight tracking.
- UI/Data: `IBM Plex Mono`, medium to bold.
- Keep type oversized and legible for a phone screen.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should feel like product evidence assembling itself.
- Use directional wipes and moving masks as transitions.
- Show progress, routing, cards, and decisions, not decorative floating blobs.
- CTA must clearly show `npx apparent`.

## What Not To Do

- Do not reuse the OpenClaw storyline.
- Do not make a static carousel.
- Do not use dark-blue SaaS gradients.
- Do not rely on tiny text to explain the product.
