# Apparent Gen Z Signal Ad

## Style Prompt

Create a fast, snappy vertical marketing video for Apparent. It should feel like a high-energy social edit: quick cuts, sticker-like text, beat-synced image pops, and bold proof-versus-pitch messaging. Keep the supplied hand-drawn illustrations as the main content and use Apparent brand colors rather than generic neon or dark SaaS styling.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Ink: `#28262a`
- Graphite: `#4a4a4c`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`
- Warm orange: `#ff9473`
- Soft cream: `#fffdfa`

## Typography

- Use generic serif for large kinetic text.
- Use the Apparent wordmark image for the final brand lockup.
- Text must be short, meme-legible, and readable on mobile.

## What Not To Do

- Do not bury the video in long explanatory copy.
- Do not use copyrighted music or external audio.
- Do not crop away important text inside the source illustrations.
- Do not use black backgrounds or old dark-button styling.
