# Apparent Proof Promo

## Style Prompt

Create a warm, hand-drawn Apparent promo video that keeps the supplied illustrations as the primary subject. The frame should feel like clean paper, soft proof-of-work fundraising, and calm founder-to-investor matching rather than a loud SaaS ad.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Ink: `#28262a`
- Graphite: `#4a4a4c`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`

## Typography

- Use the Apparent wordmark image for brand text.
- Use a generic serif only for the short closing line so the render does not depend on web font capture.

## What Not To Do

- Do not crop the source illustrations.
- Do not use dark hover-era styling, neon gradients, or black backgrounds.
- Do not add verbose captions over the artwork.
- Do not replace the mascot or redraw the supplied images.
