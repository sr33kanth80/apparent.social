# Apparent Codex Apple Ad

## Style Prompt

Create a clean, snappy vertical marketing video for Apparent with Codex-like command/interface rhythm and Apple-like product clarity. Use fresh UI-inspired assets only: glass panels, command palette, code/proof feed, thesis-fit map, and brand lockup. Do not use the old hand-drawn illustration PNGs.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f7f7f2`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Muted: `#6c6c70`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`
- Accent orange: `#ff9473`

## Typography

- Use system sans-serif for clean product UI.
- Use compact mono-style labels for command/code snippets.
- Use the Apparent wordmark image for final branding.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use old illustration assets.
- Do not make the music toy-like or playful in a childish way.
- Do not use dense paragraphs; every message must read fast on mobile.
- Do not use unrelated OpenAI, Apple, or Codex branding.
