# Apparent Voiceover Explainer

## Style Prompt

Create a vertical social explainer for Apparent using the supplied hand-drawn illustrations as the main visual material. The tone is warm, clear, founder-friendly, and investor-literate. Motion should feel like a calm guided walkthrough, not a loud ad.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Ink: `#28262a`
- Graphite: `#4a4a4c`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`
- Warm check orange: `#ff9473`

## Typography

- Use the Apparent wordmark image for brand text.
- Use generic serif for large voiceover chapter text.
- Keep text sparse because the illustrations already contain writing.

## What Not To Do

- Do not crop away essential writing inside the supplied illustrations.
- Do not add dark SaaS styling, neon gradients, or black hover-era visuals.
- Do not cover the illustration labels with captions.
- Do not introduce new mascot artwork or unrelated stock imagery.
