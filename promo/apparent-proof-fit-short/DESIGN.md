# Apparent Proof Fit Short

## Style Prompt

Create a fast vertical Apparent marketing short with reusable sticker/card assets, quick narration, and a darker current synth/drill-pop beat. The tone should feel modern and confident, not childish or toy-like. Use the supplied hand-drawn illustrations as the product-world base and the short-kit SVGs as punchy overlay assets.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Ink: `#28262a`
- Graphite: `#4a4a4c`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`
- Warm orange: `#ff9473`
- Cream: `#fffdfa`

## Typography

- Use generic serif for kinetic text and SVG cards.
- Use the Apparent wordmark image for brand lockup.
- Keep captions short enough to read in under one second.

## What Not To Do

- Do not make the music playful, nursery-like, or toy-ish.
- Do not overload the video with subtitles.
- Do not crop away essential text in the illustrations.
- Do not use black SaaS backgrounds or unrelated neon stock visuals.
