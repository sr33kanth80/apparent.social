# Apparent VC Signal Radar Promo

Vertical 1080x1920 social promo for venture investors. This is a sibling to the cracked-founders ad, but with a new narrative: another spreadsheet is not the answer; live proof signals feed a thesis radar; investors open a match, read a why-now memo, and reach out before the round is crowded.

Visual direction: crisp Apple/Codex-like interface motion, pale Apparent canvas, dark command surfaces, green/blue/orange proof accents, radar rings, ranked startup tiles, and a final brand lockup. No old hand-drawn illustrations.

Narration runs through the whole spot and closes with an Apparent outro.
