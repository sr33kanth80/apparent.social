# Apparent VC Verified Illustration Promo

Vertical 1080x1920 Instagram promo for venture investors. The spot uses editable SVG illustrations inspired by the Ian Xiaohei whiteboard style, but adapted to Apparent's green/blue/orange brand colors and English labels.

Narrative: noisy list -> verified startups -> founder/team story -> caliber/proof -> thesis fit -> conviction/outreach -> Apparent outro.

Visual rules: clean white illustration cards, dark synth-pop tech frame, snappy transitions, large mobile-readable captions, no old illustration PNGs, no crowded diagrams.
