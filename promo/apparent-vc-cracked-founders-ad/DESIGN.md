# Apparent VC Promo

Vertical 1080x1920 social ad for venture investors. The narrative is investor-first: stop browsing founder databases, define thesis, receive pre-vetted startups, inspect proof, and move to memo/outreach.

Visual direction: clean Apple-like canvas with Codex-style command surfaces, investor dashboard motion, crisp thesis cards, ranked founder/startup cards, and a final Apparent brand lockup. No old illustrations.

Narration must run through the full video and close with the Apparent outro.
