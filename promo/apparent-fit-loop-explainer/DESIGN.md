# Apparent Fit Loop Explainer

## Style Prompt

Create a vertical narrated explainer for Apparent that feels like a direct product story: founders have scattered proof, investors have noisy lists, and Apparent creates a fit loop between proof and thesis. Use the supplied hand-drawn illustrations as the main visual material.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Ink: `#28262a`
- Graphite: `#4a4a4c`
- Founder green: `#c8dfaa`
- Investor blue: `#97cde5`
- Warm check orange: `#ff9473`

## Typography

- Use the Apparent wordmark image for brand text.
- Use generic serif for sparse chapter text.
- Keep captions large and brief so they work without covering illustration text.

## What Not To Do

- Do not crop away the writing inside the source illustrations.
- Do not introduce extra characters, stock imagery, or unrelated UI mockups.
- Do not use black backgrounds, neon gradients, or old-style dark button aesthetics.
- Do not overload the visuals with subtitles; the voiceover should carry the detail.
