The Solution

Apparent turns a founder's real work into a profile — then ranks it against an investor's actual thesis, and lets two AI agents do the outreach.

✓For founders

Connect GitHub and your launch — Apparent builds a proof profile from real commits, traction, and story. No pitch deck required to get discovered.

A founder agent finds the on-platform investors whose thesis fits, drafts intros in your voice, and amplifies you to every match — once.

✓For investors

Set a private thesis — sectors, stage, geography, check size, founder signals. Apparent ranks every builder on and off the platform against it.

An investor agent sources founders who fit, drafts personalized outreach, and — if you let it — follows up, on autonomy you control.
