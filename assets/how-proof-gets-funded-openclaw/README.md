## Storyline

Founders already have proof. It is usually scattered across repos, launch pages, traction screenshots, and half-finished investor notes. Apparent makes that proof readable, maps the right investors, and turns a cold pitch into a warmer conversation.

## QA notes

- Use one frame per post or carousel slide.
- Keep captions outside the image where possible.
- Do not add a title card inside the illustration.
- Preserve OpenClaw identity: red round body, side claw-like arms, two antennae, black eyes, cyan pupils, short legs.
- Preserve sparse article-illustration style: white or near-white background, loose black linework, restrained red and cyan, generous whitespace.
- Avoid UI screenshots, terminal screenshots, code screenshots, pitch deck layouts, and dense infographic text.

## Repurposing

- Instagram carousel: use all five frames in order with captions as slide text outside the artwork.
- LinkedIn post: use frames 1, 3, and 5 as a three-image narrative.
- Blog body art: place frame 1 after the problem section, frame 3 after investor discovery, and frame 5 near the CTA.
- Founder DM asset: send frame 1 or frame 2 with a short note about turning public proof into investor-readable proof.
