# Apparent — Narrated Brand Overview

## Style Prompt
Third piece in the Apparent campaign — the calmer, fully-narrated sibling to the two hard-cut social ads. Same visual system (`--ed-*` palette, Inter Tight), but `default`-tone pacing: soft opacity crossfades, comfortable rhythm, scenes timed to match measured voiceover audio rather than an independent cut rhythm.

## Colors
- Ink (dark scenes — hook, turn, contrast, outro): `#222222`
- Canvas (light scenes): `#e9e9e9`
- Paper (card surfaces): `#ffffff`
- Ember (accent): `#fa5d29`
- Graphite (secondary text on light): `#4d4d4d`
- Smoke (tertiary text/meta): `#808080`
- Fog (hairline borders on light): `#dedede`
- White text (on dark): `#ffffff`

## Typography
- Display font: `Inter Tight` (self-hosted `.woff2`, weights 400/500/600/700) — mixed case, never all-caps.
- Headlines: 56-88px at 1080 width. Body/caption: 26-36px.

## Motion / Pacing
- 6 scenes, ~25.7s total, driven entirely by the measured per-line voiceover durations (not an independently planned rhythm).
- Soft opacity crossfades (~0.3s, no blur/scale punch) between scenes — a deliberate contrast to videos 1/2's hard cuts.
- Music held at a constant low volume (~0.14) throughout since narration spans nearly the whole runtime.

## What Not To Do
- No ALL CAPS typography.
- No hard-cut/blur/scale transitions (that's the other two videos' style — this one is calmer).
- No invented colors outside the palette above.
- No SFX that competes with or lands on top of the narration.
