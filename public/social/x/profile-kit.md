## First Thread

1/ Warm intros are a bad database for ambition.

Great builders should become visible by what they ship. Investors should find them by thesis, proof, and timing.

That is why we are building Apparent.

2/ For founders:

Create a proof profile with your launches, traction, GitHub, pitch materials, location, and capital goals.

Then see the investors who actually fit your raise.

3/ For investors:

Capture your thesis once.

Apparent ranks relevant builders by fit, freshness, geography, stage, and proof, so sourcing starts with signal instead of noise.

4/ Our bet:

The best early companies are visible before they are famous.

Apparent is built for that window.
