# Apparent — Narrated Investor Overview (v2)

## Style Prompt
Investor-only re-scope of the narrated brand overview — same visual system and calm `default`-tone crossfade pacing as the first narrated video, but every scene speaks to investors sourcing deal flow. No founder-side content.

## Colors (revised: all-light theme, new accent)
Per direct feedback, this video is now light-themed throughout (no dark scenes) and uses a new accent instead of the brand's usual ember/orange.
- Ink (text on light): `#222222`
- Canvas (scene backgrounds): `#e9e9e9`
- Paper (card surfaces): `#ffffff`
- Accent (replaces ember for this video only): `#3454d1` — a deep cobalt blue
- Graphite (secondary text): `#4d4d4d`
- Smoke (tertiary text/meta): `#808080`
- Fog (hairline borders): `#dedede`

## Typography
- Display font: `Inter Tight` (self-hosted `.woff2`, weights 400/500/600/700) — mixed case, never all-caps.
- Headlines: 56-88px at 1080 width. Body/caption: 26-36px.

## Motion / Pacing
- 6 scenes, ~26.95s total, driven entirely by measured per-line voiceover durations.
- Soft opacity crossfades (~0.3s, no blur/scale punch).
- Music held at a constant low volume (~0.14) throughout.

## What Not To Do
- No founder-side content or copy — this video is investor-only by design.
- No ALL CAPS typography.
- No hard-cut/blur/scale transitions.
- No invented colors outside the palette above.
