Apparent is a two-sided, agent-driven marketplace for venture: founders get discovered by proof of work (code, traction, product history); investors get verified, thesis-matched dealflow. It explicitly mirrors **standout.work**'s playbook ("Your AI Talent Agent", YC, agent-to-agent qualify-then-intro, kill the spam, anonymous-until-intro) but for **investing instead of hiring**.

Mapping: standout talent = Apparent founders (free side, the supply magnet); standout companies = Apparent **investors/VCs/angels, who are the paying customers**.

**Why:** Founder-facing copy (e.g. "get funded for what you build") sells the free side; the customer who pays AND the audience reading the pitch deck are both investors.
**How to apply:** Lead investor-centric in marketing/deck copy. Final approved slide-1 headline is "The startups worth backing, verified before the first call." with sub-liner "Invest in startups that match your thesis." ("Proof of work is the new warm intro." now lives on the closing slide.) User dislikes em-dashes.
